Parsed Agendas: Privacy Note

Last updated October 31, 2026.

What we collect.

When you reserve a spot, we collect your work email (required) and, only if you choose to give them, your name, your role, how you keep up with county agendas today, and what you'd pay per month. We also record which version of our Terms you agreed to and when.

Why.

To hold your founding-member spot, to email you before the first issue, and to decide what to build and how to price it. That's all.

Where it's kept.

Your answers are sent to a private Google Form in our Google account, and Google stores them for us. Only the Parsed Agendas founders can see them. The page shows only how many founding spots are taken, never who took them.

What we never do.

We never sell your information, share it with advertisers, or add you to anyone else's list. No card is collected and nothing is charged.

Emails.

We'll email you about Parsed Agendas only, mainly launch news and your founding spot. Every email lets you unsubscribe.

Deleting your information.

Email hello@parsedagendas.com and we'll delete your reservation within 30 days.

Analytics.

This page uses no analytics, ads or tracking cookies. To show the page, your browser loads fonts from Google Fonts and asks a Google service how many founding spots are taken; Google receives your IP address for those requests.

Changes.

If this note changes in a way that matters, we'll email people on the list.
